The Grammar of Arrival

Medium: Interactive Installation

Materials: Receipt printer, monitor, projection, local language model, fabric


2026 — Loods6, Amsterdam

Visitors writing

Reading the projection

The printed receipt

The Grammar of Arrival takes a language model's own internal metric—loss, how hard a word was for it to predict—and turns it into a measure of whose writing is allowed to arrive easily. Visitors sit at a terminal and write. The model, trained on billions of pages of formal, institutional English, scores the friction between their words and what it expected.

That score is projected live onto a curtain of stretched fabric, where everyone's words accumulate into a single, asymmetrical map: dense where writing matched the model's expectations, sparse and strained where it didn't. The visitor's result is also printed, receipt-style, to take home.

Whose rhythm counts as noise, and whose is written down as truth?
