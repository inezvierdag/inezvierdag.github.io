(Un)Becoming Women

Medium: Multi-channel Video Installation

Duration: 5:00 Loop


Exhibitions

2025 — Brutus Wild Summer of Art, Rotterdam

2025 — The New Current, Rotterdam

2025 — Palet Festival, Amsterdam

(Un)Becoming Women is an immersive inquiry into identity within a postfeminist landscape. Centered on an octagonal maze of one-way mirrors, the installation creates a curated disorientation where the viewer’s reflection merges with the collective gaze.

As projected visuals track a slow descent into an uncanny aesthetic, an ominous surround-sound narrative echoes the relentless pursuit of unattainable ideals.

Who are we, and who do we feel forced to become?
