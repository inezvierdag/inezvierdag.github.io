A.I. Doorbitch

Collaboration: Mathilde van der Burght

Medium: Interactive A.I. Installation

Materials: Facial recognition software, custom A.I. training model


2025 — ArtTechTour, Dordrecht

2020 — Museumnacht 010 - Het Nieuwe Instituut, Rotterdam

Clubs have long upheld stringent door policies where the "door bitch" holds sway over entry. These decisions, often based on physical appearance, create a paradox: a tool for exclusion and prejudice, yet a necessary gatekeeper for establishing safe spaces within queer and progressive nightlife.

A.I. Doorbitch explores the delicate balance between security and inclusion by removing the human element entirely. By entrusting artificial intelligence with the task of social judgement, the work reflects on the biases baked into the code of our evolving social fabric.

Who is granted entry when the enforcer is an algorithm?
