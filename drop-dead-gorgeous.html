Drop Dead Gorgeous

Medium: Video Installation

Duration: 5:00


Video

Drop Dead Gorgeous is a feminist manifesto disguised as a video installation—an invitation to introspection and a call to arms against the insidious "Girlboss" and "That Girl" trends. These cultural archetypes gaslight the subject into believing that worth is inextricably tied to productivity and conformity.

The work explores a world where achievement becomes a performance, and validation a fleeting high from external systems that magnify and diminish in equal measure.

By dismantling the capitalist agenda that defines worth through hustle, the installation questions the very authority that dictates our goals. It asks for a reclamation of agency: a world where ambition ignites from within, fueled by values we cultivate rather than trends we blindly follow.
